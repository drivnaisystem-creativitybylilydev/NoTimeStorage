# PROJECT CONTEXT — NoTime Storage

NoTime Storage is a student storage service focused on move-out pickup, summer storage, and move-in delivery.
The system prioritizes speed, predictable logistics, automation, and future scalability.
Initial launch school: Stonehill College.

Business flow:
1. Student selects storage option and quantity
2. Student schedules move-out (pickup)
3. Items are collected and stored
4. Student schedules move-in (delivery)
5. Items are delivered directly to the student's room

Scheduling rules:
- 20-minute time slots
- Operating hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- No duplicate time slots per day
- Exception: two students may share a time slot only if they live in the same hall
- Business cannot be in two locations at the same time

Logistics:
- Move-outs are picked up at the student's residence
- Each move-out takes 5 minutes maximum
- Elevator and stairs must be confirmed
- Move-ins are delivered directly to the student's room
- Students must schedule move-in at least 2 weeks in advance
- Automated reminder emails should be supported

Dorms:
- Stonehill College dorms are known and selectable
- Future schools will be added later

Data considerations:
- Student contact info
- School and dorm
- Box and item quantities
- Applied pricing tier
- Move-in and move-out scheduling
- Elevator / stairs
- Storage duration
- Payment and communication status
